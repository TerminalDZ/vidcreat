ReelForge - Voice + YAML to Finished Vertical Explainer Videos

ReelForge turns a voice recording and one simple text file into a polished vertical explainer video (1080×1920, 30 fps) ready to publish on short-video platforms such as TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. You get animated diagrams, code that types itself, word-by-word captions, sound effects, mastered audio and platform-ready MP4 files. You don't need a video editor, keyframes, a subscription or AI.

You write your script one sentence per line and describe what appears on screen and on which word. ReelForge animates everything exactly when you say it. If you re-record one sentence, everything after it stays in sync.

Who is it for?

  • Developers and educators who make technical explainer series (system design, programming, DevOps, data).
  • Arabic creators who mix English terms into their speech. Right-to-left captions keep the English words in the correct order.
  • Agencies and teams producing consistent, branded short videos at scale.
  • Trainers and course authors who turn lessons into short clips.

How it works

  1. Create an episode in the dashboard (or with reelforge new ep10).
  2. Write the script and the scenes in episode.yaml. VS Code autocompletion is included.
  3. Record your voice with any recorder, then upload WAV, MP3, M4A, AAC, OGG, FLAC, WebM or Opus.
  4. Mark cues by pressing Space at the start of each sentence while you listen.
  5. Preview the real frame synced to your voice. It reloads every time you save.
  6. Validate and render. You get a master MP4, a share MP4 sized for upload, SRT captions, a contact sheet and a ready-to-paste caption.

Key features

  • Project dashboard (runs in your browser, on your computer only): create episodes, upload recordings, mark cues, preview, validate, render with live progress, then play and open the results.
  • Cue marker: listen at 0.5×, 0.75× or 1× speed. Nudge cues by 10 or 50 ms, re-mark any line, set word-level marks, snap to voice onsets, undo, and everything saves automatically.
  • Live preview: the exact 1080×1920 frame, synced to your audio, with safe-zone overlays for each platform, element outlines, a lint panel and an error overlay that points to the YAML line.
  • 21 animated components: title, label, code, node, arrow with moving packets, chip, stamp, table, lanes, checklist, counter, meter, timeline, grid, token bucket, phone mock-up, quote, particles, “trap” interstitial, call-to-action and icons.
  • Word-timed animation: make any element enter, change or leave on a specific spoken word, for example at: "l3:'Idempotency'".
  • Arabic-first captions: word-by-word karaoke highlight, right-to-left text with English terms in the correct order, Arabic prefixes kept attached to English words, and punctuation on the correct side. English-only captions work too.
  • Professional audio: procedural sound effects on hits and transitions, voice mastered to −14 LUFS with two-pass loudness normalisation, effects ducked under the voice, and a true-peak limiter.
  • Platform-ready output: an H.264 master (CRF 18), a share file that fits the size you choose, SRT subtitles, a contact sheet, a cover frame and a publish.md with caption, hashtags and pinned comment. Every output is checked with ffprobe after encoding.
  • 13 validation rules covering schema, timing, overlaps, off-screen elements, platform UI zones, text overflow and caption length. Each issue reports file:line:column and a fix hint.
  • Themes and presets: every color, font size and motion value lives in one YAML file. Generate a complete theme from a single accent color.
  • Fast parallel rendering uses all your CPU cores. A 2-minute episode takes about 5–10 minutes on a typical 8-core computer.
  • Batch and automation: render --all, JSON output, environment-variable configuration and clean Ctrl+C handling.
  • Offline and private: everything runs on your own computer, with no account, no uploads and no per-video cost.
  • Deterministic: the same inputs always produce exactly the same frames.

Command line

reelforge studio            open the project dashboard
reelforge new <id>          create an episode from a commented template
reelforge cue <id>          mark cues while listening
reelforge dev <id>          live preview with hot reload
reelforge validate <id>     13 lint rules with fix hints
reelforge render <id>       master + share MP4, SRT, contact sheet, publish.md
reelforge snap <id> <t>     PNG frames, contact sheet, cover image
reelforge theme new <name>  generate a theme from one accent color
reelforge doctor --fix      check and fix Node.js, FFmpeg, Chromium and fonts

Requirements

  • Windows 10/11 (64-bit), macOS 13 or later, or a 64-bit Linux distribution
  • Node.js 22 LTS and FFmpeg 6 or later, both free. The installer checks for them and offers to install FFmpeg.
  • About 1.5 GB of free disk space for dependencies and the rendering browser, which the installer downloads once
  • At least 4 CPU cores and 8 GB RAM; 8 cores are recommended
  • An internet connection during installation only
  • For the dashboard and studio: a current Chrome, Edge or Firefox

Please note

  • ReelForge is a desktop tool that runs on your computer. It is not a website script, and you don't need a web server or hosting.
  • It does not generate voices, scripts or visuals with AI. You record your own voice and describe your scenes in a simple YAML file.
  • Voice recordings are not included. The example episodes come with draft cues so you can preview and render them silently, or record your own voice.
  • The preview images and demo video were made entirely with ReelForge and the bundled example episodes. All fonts used in the previews are included.
