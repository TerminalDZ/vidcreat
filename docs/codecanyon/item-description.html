ReelForge - Your Voice to Finished Vertical Explainer Videos

ReelForge turns a voice recording and one simple text file into a polished vertical explainer video (1080×1920, 30 fps) ready to publish on short-video platforms such as TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. You get animated diagrams, code that types itself, word-by-word captions, sound effects, mastered audio and platform-ready MP4 files. It is designed in a visual editor that feels like a professional design tool and video editor, but much simpler. There are no keyframes and no subscription.

You write your script one sentence per line and decide what appears on screen and on which word. ReelForge animates everything exactly when you say it. If you re-record one sentence, everything after it stays in sync.

Who is it for?

  • Developers and educators who make technical explainer series (system design, programming, DevOps, data).
  • English and Arabic creators. Arabic captions are right-to-left and keep mixed-in English terms in the correct order.
  • Agencies and teams producing consistent, branded short videos at scale.
  • Trainers and course authors who turn lessons into short clips.

How it works

  1. Create an episode in the dashboard, in English or Arabic.
  2. Design it in the visual editor. Drop components on the canvas, edit every property in the inspector and drag clips on the timeline. Clips snap to the words you speak.
  3. Record your voice with any recorder, then upload WAV, MP3, M4A, AAC, OGG, FLAC, WebM or Opus.
  4. Mark cues by pressing Space at the start of each sentence while you listen.
  5. Review the real frame synced to your voice. Every check lists a fix hint.
  6. Validate and render. You get a master MP4, a share MP4 sized for upload, SRT captions, a contact sheet and a ready-to-paste caption.

Key features

  • Visual editor
    • Canvas: zoom and pan, selection, drag and resize with smart guides, arrow-key nudging, and double-click to edit text in place.
    • Panels: a layers panel with drag-to-reorder, and a component library with drag-and-drop.
    • Inspector: every property of every component, with scrubbable numbers, theme color swatches, effects and states.
    • Timeline: multi-track, like a professional video editor, with scenes, script cues, layers, the voice waveform and sound effects.
    • Everywhere: unlimited undo and redo, a ⌘K command palette, right-click menus, keyboard shortcuts for everything, and a first-run tour.
  • Timed to your words: the timeline snaps to the words in your recording. A title set to appear on “Idempotency” stays on that word even after you re-record.
  • AI-ready (optional): a built-in MCP server lets AI assistants write scripts, build scenes, check rendered frames and render for you. It works with Claude, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Codex, Gemini CLI, Windsurf and others. Instruction files (AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, GEMINI.md and more) and ready-made configs are included.
  • Project dashboard (runs in your browser, on your computer only): create episodes, upload recordings, open the editor, validate, render with live progress, then play and open the results.
  • Cues mode: mark each sentence with Space while you listen at 0.5×, 0.75× or 1× speed. Nudge cues by 10 or 50 ms, re-mark any line, set word-level marks, snap to voice onsets, undo, and everything saves automatically.
  • Review mode: the exact 1080×1920 frame, synced to your audio, with safe-zone overlays for each platform, element outlines and every check one click away.
  • 21 animated components: title, label, code, node, arrow with moving packets, chip, stamp, table, lanes, checklist, counter, meter, timeline, grid, token bucket, phone mock-up, quote, particles, “trap” interstitial, call-to-action and icons.
  • Word-timed animation: make any element enter, change or leave on a specific spoken word, for example at: "l3:'Idempotency'".
  • Smooth, professional motion: 17 enter and 9 exit animations (reveal, spring, drop, blur, wipe, type and more), easing per element, six scene transitions (crossfade, cut, slide-up, zoom, push, blur) and slow camera moves. Everything renders with sub-pixel precision, so motion never stutters, and one click previews any animation in the editor.
  • English and Arabic captions: word-by-word karaoke highlight. Arabic captions run right to left, keep English terms in the correct order and attach Arabic prefixes to English words.
  • Professional audio: procedural sound effects on hits and transitions, voice mastered to −14 LUFS with two-pass loudness normalisation, effects ducked under the voice, and a true-peak limiter.
  • Platform-ready output: an H.264 master (CRF 18), a share file that fits the size you choose, SRT subtitles, a contact sheet, a cover frame and a publish.md with caption, hashtags and pinned comment. Every output is checked with ffprobe after encoding.
  • 13 validation rules covering schema, timing, overlaps, off-screen elements, platform UI zones, text overflow and caption length. Each issue reports file:line:column and a fix hint.
  • Themes and presets: every color, font size and motion value lives in one YAML file. Generate a complete theme from a single accent color.
  • Fast parallel rendering uses all your CPU cores. A 2-minute episode takes about 5–10 minutes on a typical 8-core computer.
  • Batch and automation: render --all, JSON output, environment-variable configuration and clean Ctrl+C handling.
  • Offline and private: everything runs on your own computer, with no account, no uploads and no per-video cost.
  • Deterministic: the same inputs always produce exactly the same frames.

Command line

reelforge studio            open the project dashboard
reelforge edit <id>         the visual editor
reelforge mcp               MCP server for AI assistants
reelforge new <id>          create an episode from a commented template
reelforge cue <id>          the editor in Cues mode
reelforge dev <id>          the editor in Review mode (checks, safe zones)
reelforge validate <id>     13 lint rules with fix hints
reelforge render <id>       master + share MP4, SRT, contact sheet, publish.md
reelforge snap <id> <t>     PNG frames, contact sheet, cover image
reelforge theme new <name>  generate a theme from one accent color
reelforge doctor --fix      check and fix Node.js, FFmpeg, Chromium and fonts

Requirements

  • Windows 10/11 (64-bit), macOS 13 or later, or a 64-bit Linux distribution
  • Node.js 22 LTS and FFmpeg 6 or later, both free. The installer checks for them and offers to install FFmpeg.
  • About 1.5 GB of free disk space for dependencies and the rendering browser, which the installer downloads once
  • At least 4 CPU cores and 8 GB RAM; 8 cores are recommended
  • An internet connection during installation only
  • For the dashboard and editor: a current Chrome, Edge or Firefox

Please note

  • ReelForge is a desktop tool that runs on your computer. It is not a website script, and you don't need a web server or hosting.
  • The video engine does not generate voices or visuals with AI. You record your own voice and design your scenes yourself. Using an AI assistant through MCP is optional and needs your own AI subscription.
  • Voice recordings are not included. The example episodes come with draft cues so you can preview and render them silently, or record your own voice.
  • The preview images and demo video were made entirely with ReelForge and the bundled example episodes. All fonts used in the previews are included.
