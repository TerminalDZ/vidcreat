# Tutorials

Each tutorial makes one short video with one [industry pack](../templates-and-packs.md): create the episode
from a template, fill in your details, check the sector's content rules, then validate, record and render.
New to ReelForge? Do the [quick start](../quick-start.md) first.

| Tutorial                                  | Pack              | Theme        | You will make                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Healthcare](healthcare.md)               | `healthcare`      | `clinical`   | A 30-second guide to a first visit at a fictional hospital            |
| [Real estate](real-estate.md)             | `real-estate`     | `estate`     | A short tour of an apartment for a fictional agency                   |
| [Ports and logistics](ports-logistics.md) | `ports-logistics` | `harbor`     | The journey of a container from ship to truck at a fictional port     |
| [Education](education.md)                 | `education`       | `campus`     | One concept explained in 45 seconds                                   |
| [Finance](finance.md)                     | `finance`         | `ledger`     | How a simple monthly budget works                                     |
| [Food and hospitality](food.md)           | `food`            | `bistro`     | A signature dish made in four steps at a fictional restaurant         |
| [Retail](retail.md)                       | `retail`          | `retail`     | How click-and-collect works at a fictional shop                       |
| [Government services](government.md)      | `government`      | `civic`      | How residents of a fictional city renew a document online             |
| [Tourism and travel](tourism.md)          | `tourism`         | `voyage`     | A three-day itinerary for a fictional coastal town                    |
| [Manufacturing](manufacturing.md)         | `manufacturing`   | `industrial` | How a part is made at a fictional plant, from raw material to the box |
| [Events and non-profits](events-ngo.md)   | `events-ngo`      | `editorial`  | A call for volunteers for a beach clean-up day                        |
| [Tech and software](tech.md)              | `tech`            | `default`    | Rate limiting with a token bucket, for backend developers             |

Every tutorial uses fictional organizations. The content rules come from the industry playbooks in
`skills/reelforge-industry-playbooks/`, which AI assistants follow as well.
