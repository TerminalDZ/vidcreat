# Education

**Audience:** students (secondary to university), parents, teachers, lifelong learners.
**Tone:** encouraging, clear, curious; one concept per episode. Arabic: MSA for formal courses,
dialect for study tips.
**Theme:** `campus` (friendly, bright accent on a calm base); fallback `default`.

## Typical episodes

| Episode                             | Blueprint                | Components                            |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| A concept in 45 seconds             | `hook` + `process-steps` | `title`, `steps`, `tree`              |
| Common mistake and the fix          | `problem-solution`       | `trap`, `chip` with `strike`, `title` |
| Study plan for exam week            | `timeline`               | `timeline`, `checklist`               |
| Two methods compared                | `comparison`             | `comparison`, `matrix-2x2`            |
| Enrollment steps (fictional school) | `process-steps`          | `steps`, `icon` (user, mail)          |
| Quote from a (fictional) teacher    | `quote`                  | `quote`                               |

## Compliance

- Accuracy first: definitions and formulas must be correct; when unsure, ask the person.
- No exam-answer leaks or real exam papers; no named real teachers or schools.
- Content for minors: no personal data requests, no links to off-platform chats.
- Attribute quotes only to fictional people or to clearly public-domain sources the person
  confirms.

script:
