# Support

Thank you for purchasing ReelForge.

## Before contacting support

1. Run `reelforge doctor` (or open the dashboard and click the status pill at the top). It checks Node.js,
   ffmpeg, Chromium and the fonts, and prints the exact fix for each problem.
2. Check **Troubleshooting & FAQ** in the offline documentation (`documentation/index.html`).
3. Run `reelforge validate <episode>`: every problem in an episode is listed with its file, line and a hint.

## How to get help

Use the **Support** tab on the ReelForge item page on CodeCanyon (or the item comments). Please include:

- your operating system and version,
- the output of `reelforge doctor --json`,
- the command you ran and its full output (add `--verbose`),
- the `episode.yaml` involved, if the problem is episode-specific.

## What support covers

As defined by the [Envato item support policy](https://codecanyon.net/page/item_support_policy):

- answering questions about how to use ReelForge and its features,
- help with installation and the reported bugs,
- fixing bugs in ReelForge and its included examples.

Support does **not** include customization work, writing episodes or components for you, or
problems caused by modified source code or third-party software (operating system, ffmpeg builds,
antivirus software).

Support is included for 6 months from the purchase date and can be extended on CodeCanyon.
Updates (bug fixes and new features) are free for all buyers; download them from your CodeCanyon
**Downloads** page.
